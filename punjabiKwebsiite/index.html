Punjabi Kitchen

Established 15 years ago, the food journey of Punjabi Kitchen is nothing short of a joy ride. With a humble beginning of mainly catering to the needs of students, we have always served our food with simplicity and love.

Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,Refined with Passion

From a humble kitchen serving college students to becoming Ranchi's most beloved dining destination — our journey is seasoned with love, spiced with dedication, and served with an uncompromising commitment to authentic flavour.
